What is Kpop Song Quiz?

Kpop Song Quiz is a music game for K-pop fans. Listen to a 30-second preview, guess the song, and climb the leaderboard. Play solo to train your ears, or create a room and battle your friends in real time.

How it works
1
Listen
A 30-second preview plays.
2
Guess
Choose or type the song title.
3
Win
The faster you answer, the more points you earn!
Features
🎧 Solo & Multiplayer
Play on your own, or create a room and invite friends with a simple code.
💿 Album Cover Challenge
Can you recognize the album from a partly hidden cover?
🎤 Your favorite artists
Girl groups, boy groups, soloists, K-hip hop and classics.
🔥 Daily challenges
Complete daily goals, keep your streak alive and earn XP.
⌨️ Two ways to answer
Pick from four options, or type the title yourself for a x3 score.
💡 Hints
Stuck? Use a hint to get a little help.
